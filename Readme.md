HouseholdKarma

A household coordination app for joint families and shared homes.

Who It’s For

HouseholdKarma is built for joint families, and it works just as well for flatmates or any group that manages a home together.

The Problem

Most households today manage groceries and chores in one of two ways:

Messaging: Family members send item requests over WhatsApp. To recall what’s needed, someone has to scroll back through days of messages.
Paper lists: Someone writes a long list with quantities and carries it to the market.

Both methods work after a fashion, but they create everyday friction:

Items get missed or duplicated, so purchases don’t match what was needed.
Costs aren’t tracked or clear.
Items can’t be checked off while shopping. WhatsApp has no check/uncheck option, and paper needs a pen you may not have at the store.
The Solution

HouseholdKarma gives the whole household one shared, organized place to plan and track what needs doing.

Key Features
Family type selection: Choose a setup that fits your household, such as Joint Family.
Shared grocery list: Add items with quantities and assign each one to a specific person (the “doer”).
Check and uncheck items: Tick things off as you shop, with no pen required.
Reminders: Set reminders for specific dates, such as buying essentials or booking a gas cylinder.
